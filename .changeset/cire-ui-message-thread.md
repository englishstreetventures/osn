`@cire/ui/message-thread` adds `MessageThread`, an enquiry thread both portals can render: the messages oldest first in segments opened by a centred time line, a 20% gold wash for your words and a hairline-edged half surface for theirs, a phone tap that shows a message's time, a right-click or Shift+F10 that opens its info with "Copy text", one tab stop moved by the arrow keys, a quiet line for an empty thread, and a one-line 34px reply box whose round send button springs in once there is something to send. `@kobalte/core` becomes a dependency, for the info menu, which loads on first use.
