Cire's error lines, standing notes, badges, tags and run-sheet flags are drawn by the shared parts. Every hand-written error line is an inline danger `Notice` with a circled icon and the word "Error" read before it; on the Classic invite's centred column the line stays centred. The palette's notes, the RSVP sheet's closed and preview notes and the sign-in and demo preview notes are small `Notice` boxes, and the palette's contrast warning is a warning. The bordered pills — the top bar's and a co-host's role, "Connected", "New", an invite section's state, "Applies immediately", a seated guest's reply, "RSVPs closed" — are outlined `Chip` pills; the category and status tags are `Chip` tags, "No longer available" outlined in red; the run sheet's flags are `Chip` tags. The role badges explain themselves on hover instead of through a `title`. The vendor portal's design notes say so.
