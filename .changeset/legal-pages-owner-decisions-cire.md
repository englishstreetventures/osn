Cire's legal pages say who sells the plans and how. The terms, refund policy and privacy notices name Cire as the seller, with Stripe taking the payment, and describe the Gold and Crimson plans rather than add-ons. The terms add a minimum age of 18 for organisers and vendors, a vendor section, GST-inclusive prices, an AUD 100 liability cap that never reduces a refund, and New South Wales law with its courts. The hosts' notice names Stripe for plans and for registry gifts, and says how long purchase, sale and refund records are kept. The guest notice gives a purpose and basis for the reply-change record, who sent each reply, seating and gifts. Privacy pages send requests to privacy@englishstventures.com; the terms, the refund policy, the refund email and the refund notice in the portal send everything else to hello@englishstventures.com. No notice says the pages load fonts from Google. The pricing section lists the envelope among what Crimson adds.
