Adds the `registry-payout-retired` template: the notice a wedding's owners get when an owner retires the Stripe account its money gifts are paid into. It names who acted, whom the holder asked to connect the next account or whose account was replaced, says guests cannot give money until an owner connects an account, and links the registry's Settings tab. Copies speak to the actor, the owner asked to connect the next account, the holder whose account was replaced, and every other owner.
