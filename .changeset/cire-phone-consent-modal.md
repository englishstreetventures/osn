"@cire/invites": minor

The first-visit privacy prompt on an invite is now a dialog the guest answers before going on, at every screen size, instead of a banner along the bottom of the screen. Once answered, nothing covers the invite's opening screen, where the gala design puts the couple's names. Escape, the back gesture and a tap outside the dialog do not close it; only an answer does. It offers "Accept all", "Reject all" and "Choose", in that order and all three alike. It opens on its heading rather than on an answer, and links to the privacy notice and the terms, which still show the prompt as a banner so a guest can read them before deciding. On those pages the banner no longer covers the end of the notice or the footer, and keyboard focus is kept clear of it.

Consent is now asked for exactly two things, each with its own switch: Pinterest moodboards and Google Maps. Both stay off until the guest allows them; until then, and after a refusal, the map card and the link to the moodboard stand in. "Choose" opens a small sheet with just those two switches and Save. The site does no personal tracking, so the preferences and analytics switches are gone; what it stores to work at all is described in the privacy notice as necessary. Guests who answered the old prompt are asked once more. A page brought back with the browser's back button checks again for an answer given on another page. On the live site the privacy choice is read only from this site's own secure cookie: a same-named cookie set by another cireweddings.com site is ignored. The hero's scroll chevron no longer moves up for a banner, since no banner sits over an invite any more.
