An owner can retire a wedding's Stripe account: `POST /api/organiser/weddings/:weddingId/registry/stripe/retire`, body `{ handTo }`. The holder hands money gifts to another owner or disconnects; any other owner may replace the account, whether or not its holder still holds an owner seat. One D1 batch records the account in the new `registry_retired_accounts` table and clears it from the settings row, each statement guarded on the account and generation the route read, on the caller owning the live wedding, and on the hand-over target being an owner named by the holder; refusals are 403 `forbidden`, 409 `stripe_account_changed` (with the settings) and 409 `handover_not_owner`. Nothing at Stripe changes, and guests cannot give money until an owner connects the next account. The route sits behind the owner gate and the per-organiser limiter, on every tier and every deployment. Every owner is mailed (`registry-payout-retired`), from a retire budget of the wedding's own; an owner the actor seated in the last day is not, unless they are the replaced holder, whose copy is sent outside the budget. Owners' settings and the refresh carry `payoutHolderAbsent` (null to editors and viewers); `GET /hosts` marks the holder's owner seat `payoutHolder: true` for owners; the gift log marks a gift paid into an earlier account. The OAuth hand-back and the purge count an unreleased retired account as held; the purge hands back its wedding's retired Standard accounts, at most two a purge; Stripe's deauthorization releases retired rows connected before it; the unmatched-refund log reads a retired row's type.
