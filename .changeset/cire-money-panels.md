The Money gifts panel lets the couple change whose Stripe account takes their gifts. The owner who connected it hands money gifts to another owner or disconnects it, and any other owner replaces it with their own, each behind a dialog that says guests can't give money until the next account can take payments and that gifts already given stay with the account they were paid into. While the account is held by no current owner, owners see why money gifts are closed and "Use your own account", and the switch can be turned off but not on; editors are told nothing about whose account it is. A switch saved on can always be turned off, an editor's included. "Check again" never calls an absent holder's account ready, and the onboarding route's 409 `stripe_account_changed` says the account changed. The hosts panel marks the holder's seat: its remove and role controls, and the holder's own step-down and leave, are `aria-disabled` with a note pointing at Money gifts, and a 409 `holds_payout_account` toasts that note and marks the seat. The gift log says under a gift paid into an earlier account that any refund comes from it. "Remove my Cire data" says on each wedding whose payout account the person holds where to hand it over, refuses the confirmed removal on such a wedding before any call, and shows the API's own words for its 409. cire-api's E2E seed gains Handover Wedding and Absent Holder Wedding.
