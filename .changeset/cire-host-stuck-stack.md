Host portal: one stuck stack. Every module with sub-tabs draws a compact view bar that sticks under the top bar — the sub-tabs, then the open view's controls — and on Guests each view's own layer sticks under it: the RSVP table's head row, the households' head strip and each household's bar, the editor's card header. The RSVP and household tables scroll with the page, and below the width their columns need their rows stack. A search, filter, sort or sub-tab change made deep in a list brings it back to the stack's foot. The section intro paragraphs on the Guests tabs are gone. The E2E seating flow checks the view bar holds under the top bar. On Guests, a reply's status is a filled mark and a word, a plus-one reads "+1 · Kai", names keep one line, provenance badges are icons, and the RSVP page's events and reply filters are chips that fill green when chosen and show the focus ring from the keyboard. Icon-only controls and the household marks share one tooltip panel for the page, the household list's "⋯" opens one shared menu, and the editor's cut-short event heads one shared popover, so a long guest list mounts no popup per household. A "What the reply icons mean" key in the RSVP bar explains the provenance icons to a host on a phone or at the keyboard. The owner's RSVP download on the RSVPs page is a download icon beside the title, as on the Households tab. The bundle guard moves to the new measured size.
