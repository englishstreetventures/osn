The Money gifts panel lets the couple change whose Stripe account takes their gifts. The owner who connected it hands money gifts to another owner or disconnects it, and any other owner replaces it with their own, each behind a dialog that says guests can't give money until the next account can take payments and that gifts already given stay with the account they were paid into. While the account is held by no current owner, owners see why money gifts are closed and "Use your own account", and the switch can be turned off but not on; editors are told nothing about whose account it is. A switch saved on can always be turned off, an editor's included. "Check again" never calls an absent holder's account ready, and the onboarding route's 409 `stripe_account_changed` says the account changed. The hosts panel marks the holder's seat: its remove and role controls, and the holder's own step-down and leave, are described by a note pointing at Money gifts. They stay usable, since the mark can be stale, and a 409 `holds_payout_account` toasts that note and marks the seat. The gift log says under a gift paid into an earlier account that any refund comes from it. "Remove my Cire data" says on each wedding whose payout account the person holds where to hand it over, sends the removal whatever the list says, and on the API's 409 shows its own words, reads the list again and marks each such wedding. The retire's body carries the `stripeAccountGeneration` of the settings the page showed, which the organiser settings now send to owners, so a page showing an account since replaced retires nothing; a hand-over from someone the row no longer names as its holder answers 409 `stripe_account_changed` with the settings. cire-api's E2E seed gains Handover Wedding and Absent Holder Wedding.
