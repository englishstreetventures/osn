Money gifts close while the wedding's Stripe account is held by someone who holds no owner seat on the wedding, or by nobody on record: guests are offered no money gift and no cash fund, and `POST /api/invite/:slug/registry/contribute` answers 409 `cash_gifts_unavailable`. Owners read the reason `payout_holder_absent`, ahead of `stripe_not_ready`; editors and viewers read the account as one Stripe has not finished with (`stripeChargesEnabled: false`, `stripe_not_ready`). `PUT /registry/settings` refuses to turn money gifts on meanwhile, 409 `payout_holder_absent` to an owner and `stripe_not_ready` to anyone else; turning them off is never refused, and the switch survives a change of account. The account's holder cannot leave, be removed or be moved below owner while they hold it: `DELETE /hosts/:osnProfileId`, `DELETE /hosts/me` and `PUT /hosts/:osnProfileId/role` answer 409 `holds_payout_account`, after `last_owner` and before `people_limit_reached`, from a guard inside each write's own statements, and a refused removal keeps the person's RSVP notice row. `cire.host.removed` and `cire.host.role_changed` count the refusal as `result: holds_payout_account`. The E2E gift-list seed records its account's holder as the wedding's owner.
