`Button` draws the portals' one pill language. The host and vendor portals set two tokens the guest site and the marketing site leave unset: `--pill-ground`, the glass track's ground (the surface at 30%), which every boxed pill but `primary` now sits on, as do the account trigger and a file input's button; and `--pill-sm-height`, which makes a small pill 28px, level with the strips and filters beside it, while the guest site's small buttons keep their 31px. A new `form` size is the 34px form-field height with md's type; `icon` is a 28px circle. A new `glass` variant is the portals' one of several: the track's look at rest, a strip choice's halfway hover, and the strip's glass in readable gold at medium weight when chosen, the rest at normal weight with each word's width held, so the choice is marked by more than colour. `choice` and `glass` now hover on the fast step and choose on the base step, and an open `quiet` button holds its hover look. A new `bar` variant is the portals' bar icon button: `quiet`'s rest; under the pointer the ground lifts to the surface at 60%, in the body ink with a gold edge; while what it opens is open, the raised surface in the body ink, which the pointer does not move. The RSVP page's reply key is `bar` at `icon`, and `round` no longer changes while a popover is open, since nothing it draws opens one. A `glass` pill whose content is more than a word builds that content once. The RSVP table's loading rows follow the shorter row.
