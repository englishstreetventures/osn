# Landing imagery

The invitation and organiser WebP files are captures of Cire's actual components,
rendered with synthetic wedding data. No customer information appears in them.
Classic and Gala use the real design-pack `InviteHeader` components; organiser
views use `ModuleSidebar` and the Overview, BudgetView, ChecklistView,
RegistryView and VendorsView components with their native styles.

Photography is drawn from the site's existing Unsplash selections:

- `photo-1519741497674-611481863552`: the sunlit couple and bouquet, used in Classic and `celebration.webp`.
- `photo-1511285560929-80b456fea0bc`: used in Gala.

Source: `https://images.unsplash.com/<photo-id>`, downloaded at 1600px width.
These remain stock photographs, not examples of Cire customers.

`cire-social.jpg` is a 1200 × 630 capture of the landing page's own hero.
Refresh product captures when the depicted interfaces change. Use synthetic
data and first-party delivery; do not connect a capture session to customer APIs.
