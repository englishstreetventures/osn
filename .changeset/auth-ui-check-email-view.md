`@osn/auth-ui` gains `CheckEmailView` (`@osn/auth-ui/CheckEmailView`, and from the barrel): the "Check your email" screen a flow shows after it emails a link, for sign-up, recovery, step-up and email change. It shows the address, kept from page translation, and what opening the link does, unlocks Resend after a wait read from the clock (30 seconds by default), and offers "Use a different email" when the host passes a handler. Where the host passes `pairingCode`, it draws that number large, in tabular figures, with a caption: the page the link opens asks the person to type it on recovery and step-up, and to pick it on sign-up and email change. Only one to six digits are drawn; the number sits outside the live region behind a hidden "Your number:" label and is hidden while the link is expired, and after a resend the announced sentence ends "Check the number again." Both buttons are 40px tall below `md`, the touch height `musubi/social/DESIGN.md` sets for phones. The sentence sits in a polite live region and an error under it as an alert of its own, cleared while Resend sends; the countdown stays out of both. Resend is busy while it sends, keeping focus, with "Use a different email" disabled. Focus moves to the heading when the view appears and when a send ends with the wait disabling Resend. Recovery's wording promises no send, since recovery answers every address alike. Nothing renders it yet.
