The Stripe stand-in makes Express accounts and their onboarding links (`POST /v1/accounts`, `POST /v1/account_links`), and answers an account it made as not yet able to take charges. `cire-registry-money` gains three sections: the payout account's holder marked in the hosts panel, whose Remove and Leave reach cire-api and are refused there; a hand-over from the Money gifts panel, sent at the generation the panel read, that frees the holder to step down; and an owner replacing the account of a holder who is no owner now, through a new Express account at the stand-in, after the Stripe routes' limiter has opened a window of its own.
