A wedding with households and no events has its guest data deleted three calendar years after the wedding was created, the bound a wedding with an event the sweep cannot date already has; until then it is kept and not counted. The sweep reaches such weddings in its one cohort query, which now reads each wedding joined to its events. A run that deletes weddings with no events logs how many at info, a count only; a wedding with no events whose creation time is not epoch seconds is kept and counted in the kept warning, whose text is now "kept weddings it cannot date". The guest, host and common privacy notices state the bound, and the hosts' notice says that removing every event from a wedding three years old deletes its guest information at the next daily run.
