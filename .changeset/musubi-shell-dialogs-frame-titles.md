The app shell holds still and keeps its dialogs across the 768px switch. While the rail's or the top bar's chunk loads, a frame of the same box holds its place, so the page does not jump 240px sideways (or 49px down on a phone) on a cold load; if the shell's chunk or the dialogs' chunk fails to load, the frame stays and says to reload, and a later load that succeeds draws the shell with working dialogs. The sign-in, create-account and profile-switcher dialogs are mounted once, above the shell switch, so one open while the window crosses 768px stays open with what was typed. Every app dialog gives focus back to the control that opened it, and each has a name for assistive technology; the profile switcher marks the active profile with `aria-current`. On a phone a dialog's sheet is as wide as the screen; `ResponsiveDialogContent` takes a `width` prop for its desktop cap and takes no `class`. A pointer click on a rail search result opens or connects it, and the results panel is drawn as the rail's menus are (8px corners, the raised surface). The phone top bar's controls are 40px tall. Discover's two columns and the organisation list's description start at 768px, the app's one breakpoint, and a test fails on any other breakpoint in `src/`'s `.ts`, `.tsx` and `.css` files. Each page names the browser tab after its heading ("Discover · Musubi").
