Every portal menu is the shared `DropdownMenu` on its accent look — the account menus, the wedding switcher, the seat menu and the household menu — with radio theme rows, the shared switch on the haptics row and muted eyebrow labels; the guest site's link menu takes the shared touch rows. Every segmented control is `@cire/ui`'s glass `SegmentedTabs`: a module's views, the Edit modes, the RSVP page's events and the vendor portal's views. The invite builder becomes four views in the module's strip — Template, Style, Content and Codes — with Content's sections a second strip in the view bar, and on a phone one glass pill and a menu of every place; the invite message moves into Codes, which editors now open, and a move between the views keeps the draft without asking. The RSVP page's reply filter is one more glass strip of radios — All and each reply with its mark and count, a reply staying chosen until All clears it — whose replies show as their marks on a phone. The vendor portal declares the spring and no longer depends on Kobalte directly. An invite builder whose read fails says so in place of the builder, and a reload that fails after an upload keeps the builder and says so beside the save button.
