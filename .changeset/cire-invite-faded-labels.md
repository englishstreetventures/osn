The invite builder's section nav drops its visibility dots. A section that
guests will not see — switched on but empty, or switched off — has its label
faded and marked with an eye-off icon instead, on the tabs and on the
narrow-layout menu trigger, and each accessible name still says which reason.
A selected tab keeps its gold wash, and its ink moves from `text-gold` to
`text-gold-ink`; a selected, hidden tab fades that ink to 80%.
