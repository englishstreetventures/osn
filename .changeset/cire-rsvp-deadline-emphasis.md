The RSVP-by date carries its own weight, and says when it is running out

The one date a guest has to act on was a quiet line above the event cards. It now reads in three states rather than two. More than a week out it invites a reply, as before. Inside the final week it says "RSVPs close soon" and draws itself as a bordered block. Once the date has passed it says so and recedes into a neutral one. Each state differs in words as well as in shape, so a guest who cannot separate the two golds still reads the difference.

The date also appears where a guest lands, under the greeting in the claim panel, as a label rather than a second copy of the same sentence.

Exactly one of the two copies announces. The events-section notice keeps `role="status"` and the id each closed Respond button describes itself by, which is what makes that reference resolve once the deadline has passed; the panel copy is an ordinary paragraph — silent because it is not a live region, not because it is hidden.

Seven days is the threshold, measured against the instant the API already resolved as the end of the deadline's day in the wedding's own zone. Both design packs render through one component, so which state looks like what cannot drift between them, while each pack keeps its own placement.
