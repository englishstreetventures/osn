Adds the `registry-payout-retired` template: the notice a wedding's owners get when an owner retires the Stripe account its money gifts are paid into. It names who acted, whom money gifts were handed to or whose account was replaced, says guests cannot give money until an owner connects an account, and links the Money gifts panel. Copies speak to the actor, the owner money gifts were handed to, the holder whose account was replaced, and every other owner.
