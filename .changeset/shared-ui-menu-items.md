`DropdownMenu` gains checkbox, radio and link rows and an `accent` look. `DropdownMenuCheckboxItem` (a trailing check, or `Switch`'s own track and thumb with `indicator="switch"`), `DropdownMenuRadioGroup` and `DropdownMenuRadioItem` (a check on the chosen row), and `DropdownMenuItem as="a"`, a real anchor that the browser follows on a click, a tap, Enter or Space, closes its menu after its own click, keeps only a web, path, `mailto:` or `tel:` address, and adds `noopener noreferrer` to its caller's `rel` whenever it opens another tab or window. `DropdownMenuTrigger` and `DropdownMenuGroup` refuse `innerHTML`, `innerText` and `textContent`, as the other parts do. `DropdownMenuContent` takes `variant="accent"`, cire's portal menu: the raised panel with the floating shadow, rows in full ink, a 10% accent wash in readable accent ink when highlighted, an inset keyboard ring, and a pop open at the app's own `--dur-fast` and `--ease-out`, with no exit. `DropdownMenuLabel` takes `tone="eyebrow"`. `DropdownMenuHandle` hands a list the menu its root holds, so the one-menu-for-a-list pattern imports nothing from Kobalte. With a modal dialog open, the panel portals into it, as `PopoverContent` does. The default look, and where it portals with no modal open, are unchanged, except in a contrast theme (forced colours): there a focused default row and a focused `treatment="pill"` trigger now draw an outline, so focus stays visible in that theme. Two new subpaths with no Kobalte in them: `@shared/ui/ui/menu-classes` holds every class the menu renders, and `@shared/ui/ui/menu-touch-classes` holds `MENU_TOUCH`, the 44px rows a surface without Kobalte's menu draws.
