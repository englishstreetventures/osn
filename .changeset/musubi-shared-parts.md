The auth views and Musubi's forms use the shared parts. A button that starts a wait shows it with `Button`'s `busy` ("Verifying…", "Creating…", "Saving…", "Sending…"): it keeps focus, draws the orb and refuses a second press, and only the button that started the wait is busy, so a resend in `Register` leaves "Verify email" alone. While a call runs, the view's other actions wait with it: Cancel, Back, "← Use a different email" and "Lost your passkey?" are disabled until it settles. The change-email form's and the step-up's buttons show their wait the same way ("Sending…", "Checking…", "Waiting for your passkey…"). A Turnstile-gated submit stays busy, not disabled, after its spent token is dropped. Discover's Connect and `/search`'s Connect and Accept name their wait, and a second press for the same person sends nothing; below `md` those and Revoke are 40px tall. Each form field is a `Field` with a plain label, 6px above its control; the views keep their own `Notice` error lines and tie them to the field. The step-up and change-email fields are the shared `Input`, the recovery codes' "I've saved these codes" is the shared `Checkbox`, and `ProfileSwitcher`'s delete sits beside its row's button instead of inside it, 40px below `md`, with the row's hover wash on the row's button alone. The labels over the code boxes and the setup key take `Label`'s classes from `labelVariants()`. "Lost your passkey?" and "Resend code" are `Button` links centred under their action; `/authorize`'s "Create one", Discover's "Search for someone" and the legal pages' contact address are inline links, underlined at rest. Working labels end in "…".
