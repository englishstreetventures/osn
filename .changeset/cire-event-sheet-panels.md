An event's details and its RSVP form are now two panels of one sheet. Event Details and Respond each open the sheet on their own panel, and a button on each panel moves to the other without closing it: the new panel slides in from its side, the sheet eases to its height, focus lands on the new heading, and an unsaved reply survives a look at the details. Instant under reduced motion. Past the RSVP deadline the details say replies have closed instead of offering the form.
