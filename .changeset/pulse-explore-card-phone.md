An explore event card that is not featured shows its time, title, venue, host and footer whole on a phone. Below 768px its media column is 112px instead of 180px, and the live tag inside it reads "Live"; from 768px up the card is as before. The time, venue and host lines wrap instead of running past the card, the time keeps its AM or PM on the same line, the venue and neighbourhood read as one line of text, the clock and pin icons keep their size, and the footer wraps when its two labels do not fit. The title still stops at two lines with an ellipsis.
