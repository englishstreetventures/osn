When a payout account's holder deletes their Musubi account, the deletion preview warns them on each wedding with another owner, without blocking, and the erasure emails each remaining owner once that money gifts need a new Stripe account. A daily sweep on a second cron trigger (04:30 UTC) hands back retired Standard Stripe accounts 120 days after their last retire, at most three a run.
