On a phone, the first-visit privacy prompt is now a dialog instead of a banner along the bottom of the screen, so once a guest has answered nothing covers the invite's opening screen, where the gala design puts the couple's names. The dialog says the same thing as the banner and offers the same three buttons, with "Reject all" first and styled exactly like "Accept all". It opens on its heading rather than on either answer. Closing it without answering (Escape, the back gesture, or a tap outside it) records nothing, and the prompt carries on as the banner until the guest decides. Wider screens keep the banner. The privacy and terms pages keep the banner on a phone too, so the prompt never stands between a guest and the privacy notice it links to.
