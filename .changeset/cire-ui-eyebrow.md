`@cire/ui/eyebrow` is new: `Eyebrow` and `eyebrowClass`, the small-caps label that leads a section, a card or a group of fields, at one size and one letter-spacing (0.16em) in two tones — `accent` in readable gold, the default, and `muted` in the secondary ink. It sets no weight, so on a guest's invite it follows the couple's body weight. `as` renders it as `p`, `span`, `h2`, `h3`, `legend` or `dt`. No screen uses it yet; the apps' hand-written labels move onto it in a later change.

The host portal's design system (`DESIGN.md` §7) lists the part.
