`@osn/auth-ui`: four states where a person signing up could get stuck now have a way forward. `CheckEmailView` takes `productName` and, for sign-up and recovery, says above Resend once its first wait ends to look in the junk or spam folder for an email about the product; `Register` passes its `productName`, and `RecoveryLoginForm` takes one, which `SignIn` passes. `ConfirmLinkPage` states each flow's wrong-number lockout as fixed copy under its instruction and on every expired page, from `LINK_LOCKOUT` (`src/link-lockout.ts`), which a new `repo` test project (`test:repo`) holds to osn-api's constants. `TurnstileWidget` names the causes of a challenge that cannot load and offers Try again, which loads the script afresh where it failed and renders the challenge again; a load unfinished after 15 seconds counts as failed; a host may add an `alternative` sentence (`SignIn` and `RecoveryLoginForm` name the recovery code); the package exports a `./TurnstileWidget` subpath. `Register` shares its live sign-up request with the browser's other tabs in `localStorage` (`osn.signup.live`), so a sign-up started again for the same address from a new tab retires it and mails at once. `@musubi/social`'s `/verify/:requestId` placeholder holds the taller ready card (592px below `md`, 562px from it). `@tools/lab` adds the TurnstileWidget stories and shows the new copy in the CheckEmailView and ConfirmLinkPage stories.
