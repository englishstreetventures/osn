Publish no postal address on Cire's privacy pages, so the draft banners come down.

`{{POSTAL_ADDRESS}}`. Each contact line names the operator, trading as Cire,

`@shared/legal`, every field the six legal pages publish is filled and none

date moves to 2026-11-27, the date the hosts' notice already carried.
