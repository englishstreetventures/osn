An explore event card that is not featured shows its time, title, venue, host and footer whole on a phone. Below 768px its media column is 112px instead of 180px, and the live tag inside it reads "Live"; from 768px up the media column and the live tag are as before. At every width the time, venue and host lines wrap instead of running past the card, the time keeps its AM or PM on the same line, the venue and neighbourhood read as one line of text, the clock, pin and avatar sit on their line's first line, and the footer wraps with the status kept flush right. The title still stops at two lines with an ellipsis.
