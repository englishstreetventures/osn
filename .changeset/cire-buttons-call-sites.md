Cire's buttons and links take `@cire/ui`'s parts. A link inside a sentence is `proseLinkClass`: underlined at rest, in the readable gold or, for a way round to another screen, the muted ink; the invite message's links to other screens are links with real addresses, and every link that opens a new tab says so in hidden words. A link that stands alone is a pill: in the organiser portal `LinkPill`, 28px, with a mark for a website, Instagram, a map or Pinterest that becomes a 28px circle in a narrow row, and a spoken "opens in a new tab"; on the guest site an `outline` pill at the 44px floor. The RSVP table's reply key and the guest sheet's close × are `Button` `round` discs. The guest sheet's Attending and Not attending, and the marketing demo's, are `Button` `choice`: the chosen answer wears a 2px gold ring, a locked pair keeps its look while a reply saves, and the pair stacks in a narrow box.
