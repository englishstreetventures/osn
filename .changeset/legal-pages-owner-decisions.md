The operator record has two mailboxes, `privacyEmail` and `supportEmail`, in place of `contactEmail`, names New South Wales as the governing law, and drops `merchantOfRecord` and `accountDataRetention`. `IDENTITY_FIELD_NAMES` lists the fields every page's draft banner covers. Musubi's and Pulse's terms add a minimum age of 13 (or the higher age a country sets), an AUD 100 liability cap and New South Wales courts, and no longer claim a reader accepted them at sign-up. Musubi's privacy notice says what the sign-up date-of-birth check and the email-verification lookup send. Pulse's pages call the reader's account a Musubi account. The Musubi site's notice no longer says it loads fonts from Google, and its terms no longer call the source code open source.
