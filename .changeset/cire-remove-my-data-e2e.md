New flow `cire-remove-my-data`: a host removes their Cire data while another person's wedding stays, after following cire's open action from Musubi's account deletion to the wedding's download. The stack's cire-api is registered with `step-up:verify` and `graph:resolve-account`, and the host portal sends people to the stack's Musubi.
