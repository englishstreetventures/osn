The privacy notices and terms say what deleting a Musubi account does: it is deleted once 7 days have passed, usually within a day of the date shown, and can be kept until then by signing in and choosing Keep my account; its handles are held for 30 days by a keyed hash; each app deletes what it holds except what others still rely on. Musubi's notice and terms name only what Settings can do (download your data, disconnect an app, delete your account), and a test fails when either names an action Settings has no control for. A Cire wedding deleted with the account is said to wait for any payment still settling. Pulse's notice says what happens to RSVPs and hosted events. The two marketing sites say where an account is deleted, and links inside the Musubi marketing site's legal text are underlined.
