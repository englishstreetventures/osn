The invite builder's Preview, below its wide layout, opens the composed invite preview with a wave instead of a modal. The default, the Gilded wave, hands a full-height frame over to the page through a View Transition cut by a feathered circle that grows from the Preview button, with the couple's gold drawn over the front, the frame's edges and a little dust; "Back to editing" runs it out again. `?preview-wave=distortion` swaps in Jakub Antalik's Distortion Waves (MIT, vendored in `@cire/waves`), which bends the form column into the preview. Both fade under reduced motion and where the browser lacks what they need; Escape goes back, and focus moves to the preview and back to the button.
