Every Bearer route that mints a step-up token, an access token, a session or an authenticator-app factor, or that writes or spends what the owner's own step-up or email change depends on, now answers `409 { error: "session_stale" }` unless the caller holds a live session: its session cookie, or its access token's `osn_sid` binding, must name an unrestricted, unexpired session of the account. The new routes are `POST /step-up/passkey/begin`, `POST /step-up/email/begin`, `/step-up/email/:requestId/status` and `/resend`, `POST /account/email/begin`, `/account/email/:requestId/resend` and `/complete`, `POST /profiles/switch`, `POST /login/cross-device/:requestId/approve` and `POST /totp/enroll/complete`. The check runs before anything is read, written or spent, so a refusal parks no challenge, sends no mail, spends no per-account cap, link or step-up token, replaces no pending email change, and mints nothing.

The route context's new `liveSession` answers the session it found: `POST /profiles/switch` binds its new token to that session, and an email change spares it from the sweep of other sessions, so a caller admitted by its `osn_sid` alone keeps its own device. `switchProfile` takes the session's hash and never mints an unbound token. `resolveLiveOrdinarySession` replaces `holdsLiveOrdinarySession`; `resolveCallerSession` is removed (use `classifyCallerSession`).
