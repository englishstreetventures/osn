Let a guest name, rename or remove their plus-one on the cire invite. A member
the couple lets bring a guest names them in the claim and welcome panel, in
both design packs, until the RSVP deadline; the guest then appears in the
Respond dialog. The household can give a plus-one's dietary requirements only
by ticking a separate box confirming the plus-one agreed. That wording and its
version live together in `@cire/dietary` (`PLUS_ONE_DIETARY_ATTESTATION`); the
API stores a plus-one's dietary data only when the reply names that version,
and stamps it on the row. A household rename clears the plus-one's dietary
answers. A consent box now opens ticked only from a record its own writer made
against the current words. The privacy notice gains a section for the person a
guest brings.
