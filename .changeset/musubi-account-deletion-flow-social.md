A person can now download their data or have a link to it emailed (Settings → Account → Your data), and delete their account from Settings → Account → "Delete account…": a page in three steps that says what happens, asks what each app's open items should become (or deletes everything), takes the handle typed and a step-up, then schedules the deletion seven days out. Until then, signing in shows one screen with the date, Keep my account, Download my data and Sign out, and nothing else of the app. An organisation that passed to someone by succession can be declined from its page, through the "you now own" email's link; the page offers it only when the API says this person may still decline it.
