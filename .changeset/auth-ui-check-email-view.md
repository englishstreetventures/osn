`@osn/auth-ui` gains `CheckEmailView` (`@osn/auth-ui/CheckEmailView`, and from the barrel): the "Check your email" screen a flow shows after it emails a link, for sign-up, recovery, step-up and email change. It shows the address, kept from page translation, and what opening the link does, unlocks Resend after a wait read from the clock (30 seconds by default), and offers "Use a different email" when the host passes a handler. Where the host passes `pairingCode`, it draws that number large, in tabular figures, with a caption: the page the link opens asks the person to pick it. Only one to six digits are drawn; the number sits outside the live region behind a hidden "Your number:" label and is hidden while the link is expired, and after a resend the announced sentence ends "Check the number again." Both buttons are 40px tall below `md`, the touch height `musubi/social/DESIGN.md` sets for phones. The sentence and any error sit in one polite live region; the countdown stays out of it. Focus moves to the heading when the view appears and when Resend disables itself. Recovery's wording promises no send, since recovery answers every address alike. Nothing renders it yet.
