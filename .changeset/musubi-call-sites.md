Musubi's screens draw from the shared parts they were copying. A signed-out page now offers a Sign in under its note, outside the note's live region, which opens the sign-in dialog; until the dialogs have loaded it stays hidden and keeps its place, so it never offers a dialog that cannot open. The organisation create and edit dialogs pad their fields 16px from the edge and put their buttons in `DialogFooter`. The organisations list's rows are `Card`s: they keep the card's fill in the dark theme, and their chevron turns to full ink on hover and press. A member's role is an outlined `Chip` reading "Admin" or "Member" rather than the API's raw value, and `/authorize`'s "Third-party app" mark is the same chip, its word kept at 500. Names in search results, Settings' profile card and `/authorize`'s account, picker and consent screens, and the "Signed in as", "People" and "Organisations" headings, take the title step at 500; the handle under Settings' profile name takes `text-meta`. "Member" on a search result is the same outlined `Chip` as the roster's. The organisations list truncates a long name or handle, and its loading placeholder is the 74px height of a loaded row. `/authorize`'s two consent marks take `text-meta`, which exists, in place of `text-caption`, which did not, and its dead-end and error boxes and its consent screen's scope rows are `Card`s. The rail's search field takes its height from `Input`. `DESIGN.md` records each of these, with the new §Chips, the form-dialog shape and the field heights.
