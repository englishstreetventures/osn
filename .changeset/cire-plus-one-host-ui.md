Let editors choose who may bring a plus-one from the Households tab: a
switch per guest, and "Allow everyone" / "Allow no one" per household.
A plus-one's row sits after the guest who brought them and is named as
theirs. Turning permission off over a named plus-one asks first, naming
them, reads the list again before removing anyone, and checks the count
the API removed. Viewer co-hosts see the switches read-only.
