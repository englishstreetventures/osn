- [x] **Invite builder section nav no longer scrolls sideways on phones** (`claude/mobile-invite-builder-submenu-017xyv`, 2026-07-30) — reported: the builder's sub-menu is a scrolling menu on mobile. The eight section tabs can't share a line below `@3xl/builder` (48rem) and the row was `overflow-x-auto`, so Closing and Message sat off the right edge with nothing to say so — the same failure the module strip had before the 2026-07-22 redesign replaced it with a sheet. Below that threshold the tabs now collapse behind a **trigger naming the current section** (label + `n/8` position + its Shown/Hidden dot, so the menu only has to be opened to move, never to orient) and open as a **two-column grid** showing all eight at once: ≈206px tall on a 390px phone, nothing scrolls, 44px touch targets, absolutely positioned against the sticky bar so opening it overlays the form instead of shoving it down. Deliberately **one** tablist re-laid-out by a container query, not a second copy of the tabs in a dialog: each `SectionCard` panel's `aria-labelledby` points at `${id}-tab`, so duplicating them would give every panel two candidate labels and AT two tabs widgets (collapsed, `display: none` is fine — accname follows `aria-labelledby` into hidden subtrees). Selecting closes the menu and hands focus back to the trigger (collapsing takes the focused tab to `display: none` and focus with it); Escape, an outside `pointerdown` and focus leaving the nav also close it; `ArrowDown`/`ArrowUp` step by the grid's column count while open and are left to the browser on the wide row. Verified in headless Chromium against the built stylesheet: the crossover is exact (container 766px → trigger, 770px → row) and at the moment the row appears all eight pills plus "Preview" fit one line with room to spare. **The three pre-merge review gates found six real defects between them, all fixed on branch and each pinned by a test verified to fail with its fix disabled** — the test review's three gaps (**T-U1/T-U2/T-S1**: the trigger's dot never asserted, Escape-on-trigger uncovered, and nothing pinning the single-tablist invariant the whole a11y design rests on), the perf review's stale-state focus bug (**SM-P-I1**, in `[[perf]]` — a menu opened narrow survived the crossover and made every wide tab click drop focus to the body) plus a memo (**SM-P-I2**), and the security review's four compliance findings (**SM-C-M1/L1/L2/L3** in `[[security]]` — an open menu obscuring the field focus tabbed into, vertical arrows swallowing page scroll on the wide row, arrows stepping along the grid instead of down it, and the Shown/Hidden state being visual-only). See `[[invite-builder]]`.
