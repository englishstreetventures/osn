Account deletion reaches every app through one contract. `GET /account/deletion-preview` asks each app (`POST /internal/account-deletion-preview`) what it holds beside the organisations the account owns, and names any app it could not check; `DELETE /account` takes `delete_everything`, answers 409 `could_not_check` while an app cannot be asked and 409 `stale_choices` for an answer to an app action the fresh preview does not list, and stores app answers on the job. Once the window has passed, the fan-out sends every app (`POST /internal/account-deleted`) the person's answers to its actions and the delete-everything flag, and their email address only to an app whose entry says it receives it (neither Pulse nor Zap), for every account, a job written with the old `-1` marks included, and stores what each app kept in `deletion_jobs.app_outcomes`. The preview and the session end carry no account id. The fan-out reads each job once, marks a skipped app in its claim, counts an app done only when its own write set the mark, and stamps any job whose fan-out fails (`fanout_failed_at`), which is then read after the others. A deletion of everything stores no answers, and two triggers on `deletion_jobs` refuse the pair; the preview and erasure timeouts are part of the deletion's app settings; `OSN_DELETION_FANOUT_MAX_JOBS` caps the jobs and hard deletes per run; an app is skipped only on a local run without its URL or where `OSN_DELETION_ABSENT_APPS` declares it, a deployed tier with neither holds the deletion, and an app URL that is not `https:` (or, locally, `http:` to a loopback host) fails the boot. The soft delete ends the person's Pulse web sessions. `osn.account.deletion.fanout_oldest_age` records each app's oldest unfinished job.

osn-api publishes each isolate's outbound ARC public key in `arc_outbound_keys` and serves the set at `GET /.well-known/arc-jwks.json`; the registration it pushed to each app is gone. Each call signs with the key it published, and concurrent first calls share one write of it. A deployed osn-api needs `INTERNAL_SERVICE_SECRET`, which keys the MAC its outbound key ids carry when it is at least 32 characters long. `@osn/client`'s deletion preview carries each app's actions and `could_not_check`, and `deleteAccount` takes `deleteEverything`.
