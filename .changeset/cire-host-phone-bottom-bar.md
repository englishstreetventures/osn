On a phone, the host portal's modules move from a drawer opened by a bar at the top of the page to a bar fixed to the foot of the screen: Overview, Guests, Events, Invite and More. More opens every module as a grid of tiles that fits one screen, and names the open module when the bar does not hold it. Save bars, the invite builder's narrow preview and toasts stand on the bar.
