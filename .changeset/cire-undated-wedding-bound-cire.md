A wedding with an event whose date the guest-data sweep cannot read has its guest data deleted three calendar years after the wedding was created, whatever its other events' dates; a wedding whose dates all read keeps the one-year rule. Each run logs how many weddings it deleted under this bound, a count only. The parting gift email says which rule applied and the day it counted from, the host portal's gift record names no period and links the hosts' notice, and the guest, host and common privacy notices state the new bound.
